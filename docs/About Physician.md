About Dr. Bruce Millman

Bruce Millman is a board-certified pulmonary and critical care physician with more than 25 years of experience helping patients breathe better and live healthier lives. His practice is built around a patient-first philosophy that focuses on education, compassion, accessibility, and personalized care.

Known for taking time with patients and explaining complex conditions in simple, understandable language, Dr. Millman has earned a strong reputation for delivering a more human-centered healthcare experience. His calm, modern practice environment and highly attentive approach are designed to reduce stress and help patients feel comfortable from the moment they walk in.

Dr. Millman specializes in:

Asthma
COPD
Chronic cough
Shortness of breath
Sleep apnea
Lung nodules
Oxygen management
COVID recovery
Pulmonary and respiratory conditions
Critical care medicine

He completed his medical degree at Michigan State University College of Osteopathic Medicine (Class of 1996), followed by residency training in Internal Medicine and fellowship training in Pulmonary and Critical Care Medicine at Henry Ford Hospital (1999-2002).

Dr. Millman serves as an Assistant Professor of Internal Medicine at the Oakland University William Beaumont School of Medicine. He is affiliated with Corewell Health William Beaumont University Hospital and DMC Harper University Hospital. He has been recognized multiple years in a row by physician peers in Hour Detroit Magazine as a "Top Doctor" for excellence in respiratory disease care.

With offices in Clawson and West Bloomfield, the practice combines advanced pulmonary expertise with modern telemedicine capabilities, making care more accessible and convenient for patients across the region.
